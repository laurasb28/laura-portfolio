My content framework

Beautiful is the start. Results are the goal.

Each video follows a structure that turns attention into action.

  1. 01

    Hook

    Stop the scroll in the first seconds with the most striking moment.

  2. 02

    Context

    Show the moment, the need or the place, and who it’s for.

  3. 03

    Experience

    Use it for real: how it looks, feels and works.

  4. 04

    Highlight

    Point out the detail that makes your brand different.

  5. 05

    CTA

    Tell viewers what to do next: save, buy, book or visit.

Case study · UH Travel

From 0 to 1,500 followers in 3 months

UH Travel is my agency in Rio de Janeiro, planning custom itineraries and booking tickets for visitors to Brazil. I started its social account from zero and created all of the content myself.

Because I talk to travellers every day, I knew the questions they ask before booking a trip. I turned those questions into short videos that answered them, and the content resonated with travellers. The account grew from 0 to 1,500 followers in its first 3 months, and its videos have since reached 1.4 million views and more than 26,000 shares.
